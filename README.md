## The pitch

Knockr lets homeowners hire a vetted stranger to knock on their own front door and wait on the porch, so they can experience the thrill of an unexpected visitor they fully paid for and scheduled themselves.

One sentence, 206 characters: a real-sounding product, a founder who means every word, and a service nobody on earth needs — paying a stranger to surprise you with a visit you arranged yourself.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is needed — no network, no secrets, no dependencies beyond the Python standard library.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
